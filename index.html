How it works

Four steps, one public record

  1. Unlock

    Eligible Credail members unlock a first allowance of 25,000 ii, set by a starting ii Score of 25.

  2. Mint

    Mint any amount within your allowance, whenever you choose. Nothing is minted automatically.

  3. Return

    Each mint comes with a short series of voluntary returns. The exact schedule will be published before launch.

  4. Record

    Every return made — and every return not made — stays on your public record and sets your ii Score.

The ii Score

Your allowance is set by your ii Score

  • 0–100 The ii Score starts at 25 and is separate from your Credail score.
  • × 1,000 Your allowance is your ii Score × 1,000 ii, up to 100,000 ii.
  • public Returns made on schedule build the score over time; returns not made lower it.

How the score rule works

With every return made on schedule, a member's score is designed to reach 50 after the first year and 100 after ten years.

Questions

The rules, plainly

These describe the planned rules, which may change before launch.

What is ii?

ii (say “use”) is a rules-based token in development for members of Credail. Members will be able to mint ii within an allowance set by their ii Score, make voluntary returns, and build a public record.

Is ii a loan?

No. Minting ii creates no debt and no obligation to repay, and there is no interest. Returns are voluntary; they affect your ii Score and your future allowance.

Does ii have a dollar value?

ii has no guaranteed dollar value. No one promises to redeem ii, buy it, or exchange it for money, and there may be no buyer at any price.

Who will be able to mint?

Eligible Credail members who pass the planned checks, including a verified phone number and device checks.

What happens if a return is not made?

It stays on your public record and lowers your ii Score, which lowers your future allowance. Under the planned rules, nothing you already hold is taken away.

Why is the score so precise?

Scores are designed to be calculated exactly and kept to eight decimal places, so anyone can check every result once the rules are public. They are shown rounded.

Is there a sale, presale or airdrop?

No. There is no ii sale, presale or airdrop, and we will never ask for your keys or recovery phrase.

When will ii launch?

There is no launch date yet. This page will be updated.
